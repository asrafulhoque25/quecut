Role Summary

We seek a Junior UI Designer to help us create intuitive and engaging interfaces. You will collaborate with senior designers and product managers to enhance user experience across our platforms. If you're passionate about design and eager to learn, join our innovative team!

Perks & Benefits

  • Competitive starting salary with performance-based bonuses
  • Flexible remote work options and global collaboration opportunities
  • Comprehensive health, dental, and vision plans
  • Access to the latest design software and continuous professional development
  • Opportunity to contribute to diverse projects in cutting-edge industries
  • A vibrant, inclusive culture that values creativity and teamwork

Key Responsibilities

  • Design user-friendly interfaces for web and mobile applications
  • Work with cross-functional teams to ensure design consistency
  • Assist in developing and maintaining UI style guides
  • Refine visual elements to improve user engagement
  • Support design projects from concept to completion
  • Research and implement modern design trends
  • Prepare design assets for digital and print use
  • Participate in brainstorming and design reviews

What You Need

  • Bachelor's degree in UI Design, Interaction Design, or related field
  • 1+ years of UI design experience (internships included)
  • Familiarity with design tools (Figma, Sketch, Adobe XD)
  • A portfolio showcasing your design skills and creativity
  • Basic understanding of design principles and usability
  • Basic knowledge of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript is a plus
  • Good interpersonal skills and openness to feedback
  • Ability to manage time effectively and meet deadlines
