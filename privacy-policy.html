Such as your name, business name, email address, phone number, job title, and any details you provide when filling out forms, requesting audits, or contacting our team.

5. Data Retention

We keep your personal data only as long as necessary to:

  • Deliver services

  • Resolve disputes
  • Maintain business records
  • When data is no longer required, we securely delete or anonymize it in accordance with industry standards.

6. How We Protect Your Information

We use multiple layers of security to keep your data safe:

  • Encrypted website connections (HTTPS)
  • Secure data storage systems
  • Access control and authentication measures
  • Regular security audits and monitoring
  • Limited employee access to sensitive information
  • While no system is 100% invincible, we work hard to maintain a secure environment and quickly address any issues.

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we store about you

  • Request deletion of your data
  • Restrict processing in certain situations
  • Object to certain types of data use

  • Opt-out of marketing communication anytime
  • To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not intended for individuals under 13 years old (or the minimum age defined by local law). We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If we discover such data, we delete it immediately.

9. International Data Transfers

If you are accessing PexNet from outside the country where our servers are located, your information may be transferred internationally. We take appropriate safeguards to ensure your data remains protected.

10. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external sites like partner pages, resources, or tools. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites. We recommend reviewing their policies before sharing information.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When updates are significant, we will provide notice on our website or reach out through email. All changes go into effect once posted.
