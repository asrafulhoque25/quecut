How much should a brand identity cost in 2026?

Packaging 15 July Last updated: 28 July

The retail landscape is evolving fast, and packaging design sits at the center of that shift. Consumers now make purchasing decisions in seconds, often based on how a product looks on the shelf or in an unboxing video. For brands competing in crowded markets, packaging is no longer just a container — it's a storytelling tool, a sustainability statement, and a sensory experience.

Content with image

Sustainability has become the single most influential force in packaging graphics. Over 70% of consumers say they prefer brands that use eco-friendly packaging, and this preference is now directly shaping visual design choices across the industry.

Content layout

Sustainable packaging is no longer limited to swapping plastic for cardboard. The graphics industry is embracing plant-based inks, water-soluble coatings, and mono-material constructions that simplify recycling. Designers are rethinking color palettes to work with unbleached substrates, using kraft textures and earthy tones that signal environmental responsibility.

Check style

Structural packaging design is experiencing a creative renaissance. Brands are moving beyond standard box formats to create shapes that demand attention on the shelf and deliver memorable unboxing experiences for e-commerce customers.

Here are the key structural innovations transforming retail packaging:

  • Die-cut windows: Custom die-cut openings let consumers see the product inside, building trust and reducing the need for excessive product photography on the box.
  • Modular packaging: Interlocking and stackable designs allow products to be displayed together as a collection, increasing visual impact at retail.
  • Tactile finishes: Soft-touch coatings, embossed logos, and spot UV varnishes add a physical dimension that flat print cannot achieve.
  • Compact engineering: Brands are reducing void space with precision-fit inserts and folding structures that lower shipping costs and minimize material waste.
Tips

Start with the most impactful material and finish choices, then build a packaging system that scales across your product line while maintaining visual consistency.

Number style

Personalized and interactive packaging is the third major trend reshaping retail this year. Advances in digital printing technology have made short-run, variable-data packaging economically viable for brands of all sizes.

These are the core techniques driving personalized packaging forward:

  1. Variable data printing
  2. QR codes, AR experiences, & smart labels
  3. Limited edition graphics
  4. Regional & seasonal variants

Bullet point style

Digital printing has unlocked a new era for packaging graphics. Brands can now produce unique designs for individual SKUs without the cost burden of traditional plate-based printing, enabling true mass customization.

These are the main advantages of digital-first packaging workflows:

  • Variable data printing
  • QR codes, AR experiences, & smart labels
  • Limited edition graphics
  • Regional & seasonal variants
